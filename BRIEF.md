# Colossus (working brief)

One player can be a 2000 ft giant who roams the map slowly; the others play a standard RPG as humans, with quests, monsters and shops.
Humans can climb the giant and build on its body: weapons that turn it on rival humans and towns, or works that weaken and cripple it until it falls.

The game is built on this branch once the owner settles whether it starts fresh or continues Colossus Roams, and whether play is online.
